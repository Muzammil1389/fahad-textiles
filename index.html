FAQ

Questions we hear often

Is there a minimum order quantity?

We accept small orders for embroidery and printing. Bulk pricing starts at larger quantities. Tell us your quantity and we will quote accordingly.

How long does an order take?

Samples are usually ready within a few days. Production time depends on quantity and complexity, and we confirm a delivery date with your quote.

What file format should I send for my logo?

A vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG) gives the best result. A clear, high-resolution PNG or JPG also works. We digitize the design for embroidery at no extra cost.

Can you supply the garments as well as the embroidery?

Yes. We can source shirts, polos, caps, jackets and uniform fabric, or work on garments you provide.

Do you deliver outside your city?

Yes. We ship orders across Pakistan through trusted courier partners. Delivery charges are shown in your quote.

Ready to put your brand on fabric?

Send your design and quantity. We reply with a quote, usually the same day.
